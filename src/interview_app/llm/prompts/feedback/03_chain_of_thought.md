Technique: chain-of-thought, implemented as an evidence-first analysis: you write a structured analysis of the CANDIDATE'S ANSWER before you score it, so every score is grounded in quoted evidence.

Fill the "analysis" field first, as a short structured paragraph covering, in this order:
1. What the question is testing (the underlying competency).

3. What a strong answer would include that is missing or wrong.
4. Which evidence supports each dimension score.

Then decide the scores (1-5 each for correctness, depth, structure, communication), the overall score (1-5), 2-4 strengths, 2-4 gaps, a model answer (max 200 words, spoken style) and one follow-up question.
